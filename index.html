Who We Are

JLP Home Services is a locally owned and operated home improvement company proudly serving Nashville, Clarksville, and Franklin, Tennessee. We believe your home should be a place of comfort, beauty, and functionality, and we bring integrity, craftsmanship, and genuine care to every project.

Our mission is simple: to deliver high-quality workmanship with honest communication and dependable service. Whether it’s a small repair or a major renovation, we approach each job with the same attention to detail and dedication to excellence. We take pride in treating your home like it’s our own.

What sets us apart is our commitment to personalized service. We understand that no two homes — or homeowners — are the same. That’s why we take the time to listen to your needs, offer clear guidance, and ensure the final result matches your vision.

Serving our community is at the heart of everything we do. We're proud to be the trusted choice for home services in the area, and we’re grateful for the continued support of our loyal customers. When you work with JLP Home Services, you're not just hiring a handyman — you're gaining a reliable partner who’s here to help your home thrive.

What We Do

We provide a comprehensive range of handyman services, addressing everything from minor repairs to larger home improvement projects. Our skilled team is ready to tackle your to-do list, ensuring your home is safe, functional, and looking its best.

Deck Building Expertise

Custom deck design, construction, repair, and maintenance.

Landscaping

Painting
